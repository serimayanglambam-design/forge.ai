Annual leave
Each employee receives 20 paid annual-leave days per complete calendar year. For employees who join or leave during the year, the allowance is prorated by completed months of service. Request planned leave at least ten working days in advance through the staff leave portal. Include your dates and number of days; your manager will consider team coverage and respond there. A request is not approved until the portal confirms it. Carry unused days forward only where local law requires it or the department head approves a written exception.

Sick leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid sick-leave days per calendar year without a medical certificate. Tell your manager as soon as practical when illness prevents you from working, then record the absence in the portal when able. For an absence longer than five days, contact People Operations about supporting documents. Sick leave is for recovery, not a substitute for annual leave.

Maternity leave
Eligible employees may receive a 10-day paid maternity top-up in addition to their statutory maternity benefit. Contact People Operations before the expected leave date to confirm eligibility, pay arrangements, and required documents. Record the top-up separately from annual and sick leave.

Applying and questions
Use the staff leave portal for annual-leave requests and sick-leave records. Include clear start and return dates. In urgent situations, notify your manager directly, then update the portal as soon as possible. People Operations can explain balances and help with portal access. This policy does not replace local law; where the law provides a greater entitlement, the legal entitlement applies.
